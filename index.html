About

Art, printing and intent.

InkyBoo Print Studio is a modern print shop in Sri Lanka specialising in personalised printing, business printing and creative products.

We believe every print should feel premium, whether it is a single photograph, a business card or a custom gift. Our goal is high-quality printing with fast turnaround and friendly service.

Our mission is to make professional printing affordable, accessible and enjoyable for everyone, whether you are preserving memories or growing a business.
